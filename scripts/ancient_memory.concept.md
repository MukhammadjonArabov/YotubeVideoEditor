# How Did Ancient Humans Remember EVERYTHING Without Writing? (13 daqiqa)

**Nega:** raqobatchilarning eng ko'p ko'rilgan videolari kundalik hayot savollari ("What Did Ancient Humans Actually Do All Day?" 8.8M). Bu mavzu shu formatda, lekin tomoshabinga amaliy foyda beradi: oxirida u "xotira saroyi" usulini o'zi sinaydi. Kanalda bunday video yo'q (video_check: yaqin, takrorlanmaydi).

**Hook:** savol bilan boshlanadi ("what did you eat three days ago?"), keyin 300 000 yil yozuvsiz, 7 000 yillik hikoyalar, telefonga tayanish (Sparrow 2011), va'da (6 haftada eslab qolish ikki baravar, Dresler 2017), ochiq sikl: Gretsiyadagi falokat (Simonid).

**Tuzilma:** hook, yozuvsiz dunyo, nega bugun unutamiz, songlines (Nunn & Reid 2016, bahsli), Simonid va method of loci, og'zaki dostonlar (Iliada, Parry & Lord), xotira chempionlari (Maguire 2003, Dresler 2017), amaliy qism: 5 qadamda xotira saroyi, Stonehenge gipotezasi (Kelly, gipoteza deb aytilgan), yakun.

**Monetizatsiya:** o'z burchagi va hikoya yoyi bor; tibbiy, huquqiy, moliyaviy maslahat yo'q (xotira usuli o'quv mashqi sifatida).
