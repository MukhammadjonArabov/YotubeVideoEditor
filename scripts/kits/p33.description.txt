One extra negative word in a headline gets more clicks. Your skin and pulse react to bad news before you've even decided what you think.

By the end, you'll know where this pull came from (snakes, campfires and the smoke detector principle), why one insult outweighs ten compliments, the surprising group whose brains barely have it, and how to notice the alarm without obeying it.

This video is psychology and history, not medical or mental-health advice.
