Quick: what did you eat three days ago? For almost all of human history there was no writing, yet people carried maps, laws, stories and survival knowledge in their heads. How did they remember everything?

This video follows how: Aboriginal songlines, the Greek poet Simonides, oral epics, and what modern brain science says about the memory trick they shared. Then you build your own memory palace in under a minute.
