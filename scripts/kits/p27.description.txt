Meet a child who can see clearly underwater. This video asks what ancient and traditional ways of life could do that most of us can't, and which of those skills you can actually regain.

By the end, you'll know which skills you can regain: sea nomads' eyes and spleens, Twa tree climbers, the Hadza gut, the Tsimane heart, Kalahari trackers, scent trails and cold sleeping.

This video is history and science, not medical advice.
