Fresh meat goes bad in just days. Yet in a Chinese desert, researchers found cheese buried about 3,500 years ago. How did ancient humans beat rot, thousands of years before the fridge?

This video follows six ancient tricks: drying, salting, sweet and sour preserving, smart storage, cold, and fermentation. It covers a 500-year-old Inca potato, 8,000-year-old salt works, 11,000-year-old granaries, a Persian ice house in the desert, and the surprising science of fermentation.

This video is history and science, not food safety advice.
