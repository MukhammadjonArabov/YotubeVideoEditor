Half of all Stone Age children never grew up. Yet many adults lived past seventy, moved more than we do and had more free time.

Eight rounds, real studies of today's hunter-gatherers (Hadza, Tsimane, Agta, San) against today's world numbers, and an honest final score. Keep count with me: one round flips what nearly everyone believes.

This video is history and science, not medical advice.
