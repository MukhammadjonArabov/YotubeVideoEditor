You wake up in the Stone Age. Nobody's coming. Could you last twenty-four hours? We test it hour by hour with research on living hunter-gatherers like the Hadza.

By the end, you'll know exactly how you'd do: water, fire, food, danger, sleep, and what really shortened life in the past.

This video is history and science, not survival or medical advice.
