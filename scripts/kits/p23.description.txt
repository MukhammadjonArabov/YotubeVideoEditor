Thirty-one thousand years ago, someone amputated a child's leg. The child did not die. Who did it, and how was it possible?

This video follows the skeleton from Liang Tebo cave in Borneo, the evidence that the wound healed, the earlier record from France, early skull surgery, and the scientists who argue it was not surgery at all. By the end, you'll know how it was possible, and why researchers still disagree.

This video is history and science, not medical advice.
