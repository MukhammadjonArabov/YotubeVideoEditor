What if a Stone Age person walked down your street? This video uses real studies to imagine their reactions to crowds, light, sitting, boredom, social rejection, trade and jewelry, and to find out what they might actually envy.

By the end, you'll know what they'd actually envy. We look at the shaky "one hundred and fifty friends" rule, a camping study of body clocks, hunters who sit for ten hours, people who preferred electric shocks to quiet thoughts, and the shell beads of Taforalt.

This video is history and science, not medical advice.
