A Neanderthal who couldn't hear and had lost part of an arm lived into his forties, 50,000 years ago. Who kept him alive?

Four skeletons, one question: did ancient humans care for the injured? This video follows Shanidar 1, a toothless hominin from Dmanisi, a sick child from Sima de los Huesos, and a Neanderthal from El Sidrón who may have chewed a natural painkiller, and what their bones say about us.
