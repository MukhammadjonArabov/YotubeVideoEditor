Race a horse over twenty-two miles. You might win. This video explains why humans can outlast almost any animal over distance, and why scientists still argue about how our ancestors used it.

By the end, you'll know how springs in your legs, sweat on your skin and a very old chase-hunting idea fit together, and what the critics say. We cover the Man versus Horse marathon in Wales, the Kalahari hunters, the Turkana Boy skeleton and the wolf connection.

This video is history and science, not medical or fitness advice.
