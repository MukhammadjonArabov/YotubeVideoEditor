Your brain is probably smaller than a Stone Age hunter's. This video follows the skull evidence, the debate about when and why human brains shrank, and what brain size does and doesn't tell us.

By the end, you'll know what scientists really think: the three leading suspects (energy, shared knowledge and a changing lifestyle), the critics, Neanderthal eyes, the tiny-brained Homo floresiensis, and why a smaller brain doesn't mean a dumber one.

This video is history and science, not medical advice.
