Rain sounds cozy from a modern bed. For most of human history, a rainy night was one of the most dangerous nights there was.

By the end, you'll know the five rules that kept sleeping people alive through a storm: why sleep switches off your best heating system, why wet beats cold, the flood that comes from rain you never saw, and the 200,000-year-old grass beds of Border Cave.
