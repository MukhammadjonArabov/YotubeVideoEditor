Her arm bones were stronger than a rower's. This video asks whether ancient humans were really stronger than us, and the honest answer depends on what "stronger" means.

By the end, you'll know who really wins: we look at bone studies of early farmers and foragers, the Neandertal debate, the chimp strength myth, grip strength today and the heaviest deadlift on record.

This video is history and science, not medical or fitness advice.
