Minus twenty degrees. No heater, no jacket shop, no rescue. Twenty thousand years ago, ice sheets buried Canada and northern Europe, and ancient humans still lived beside them. How did they survive?

This video follows four survival tricks: fire, food, clothes and shelter. It covers Neanderthal fire-making, how many calories a Neanderthal may have needed in winter, a fifty thousand year old needle from Siberia, and houses built from mammoth bones.
