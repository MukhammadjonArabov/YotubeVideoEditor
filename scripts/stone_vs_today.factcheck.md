# Fact-check: Stone Age vs Today: Who Really Lived BETTER?

Every fact scene, its source, how it was checked and how the wording was kept honest. Checked 2026-10-29.

## Method
- Every study was found online and its abstract, release or a reliable summary opened; contested points are told as such.
- The video says out loud that modern foragers are not living ancestors and that one group cannot stand for all prehistory.
- No health advice: rounds describe what studies found; the ending says 'borrow the good parts' as scientists' framing, not a recommendation.

## Sources and checks

### Volk & Atkinson (2013). Infant and child death in the human environment of evolutionary adaptation. Evolution and Human Behavior 34, 182-192
- How verified: Searched 2026-10-29; paper record (Evolution and Human Behavior 34(3)) and Yale HRAF hypothesis notes: 20 hunter-gatherer and 43 historical populations; average infant mortality about 26.8% (death before 1), child mortality about 48.8% (death before 15).
- Wording safeguard: Said as 'about one in four babies', 'close to half before fifteen', 'on average across the groups studied'.

### UN IGME (2024). Levels and trends in child mortality, report 2024 (2023 global under-five mortality 37 per 1,000)
- How verified: Searched 2026-10-29; UNICEF data page and UN IGME 2024 report: global under-five mortality 37 per 1,000 live births in 2023 (range 35-41), about 4.8 million deaths.
- Wording safeguard: 'About four in a hundred, worldwide, before age five'; different age cut-offs are said out loud.

### Gurven & Kaplan (2007). Longevity among hunter-gatherers: a cross-cultural examination. Population and Development Review 33, 321-365
- How verified: Searched 2026-10-29; paper PDF on UCSB site (abstract and framing) and secondary discussion: modal age of adult death about seven decades, adaptive life span 68-78; low averages at birth are driven by child deaths; illness is the main cause of death in these groups.
- Wording safeguard: 'Around seventy' (modal, not average); the exact 72 figure is not used.

### United Nations, World Population Prospects 2024 (global life expectancy at birth 73.3 years in 2024)
- How verified: Searched 2026-10-29; UN WPP 2024 key messages and press release: global life expectancy at birth 73.3 years in 2024.
- Wording safeguard: 'About seventy-three, worldwide average at birth'; the narration notes it is a different measure from the forager modal age.

### Dyble et al. (2019). Engagement in agricultural work is associated with reduced leisure time among Agta hunter-gatherers. Nature Human Behaviour 3, 792-796
- How verified: Searched 2026-10-29; Cambridge University release and Nature Human Behaviour abstract: Agta adults about 24 h/week out-of-camp work, 20 h domestic chores, 30 h daylight leisure; mostly-farming camps about 30 h/week out-of-camp work vs about 20 h for foragers; women in farming camps had about half the leisure; co-author warns against extrapolating to prehistory.
- Wording safeguard: 'About twenty hours', 'one group in the Philippines', the caution is said.

### Raichlen et al. (2017). Physical activity patterns and biomarkers of cardiovascular disease risk in hunter-gatherers. American Journal of Human Biology 29, e22919
- How verified: Searched 2026-10-29; AJHB abstract: 46 Hadza adults with heart-rate monitors, about 135 minutes a day of moderate-to-vigorous activity; press releases give a lower ~75-minute figure.
- Wording safeguard: 'Over two hours a day, by heart-rate monitor'; compared with the 150 minutes a week guideline.

### Kaplan et al. (2017). Coronary atherosclerosis in indigenous South American Tsimane: a cross-sectional cohort study. The Lancet 389, 1730-1739
- How verified: Searched 2026-10-29; Lancet abstract via repositories and TCTMD/CBC coverage: 705 Tsimane adults, 85% had a coronary calcium score of zero, the lowest of any population recorded; observational; high burden of infection and intestinal worms, most common age of death about 70.
- Wording safeguard: 'Lowest ever measured', 'this is a link, not proof', the worms are said.

### Yetish et al. (2015). Natural sleep and its seasonal variations in three pre-industrial societies. Current Biology 25, 2862-2868
- How verified: Searched 2026-10-29; Current Biology highlights, UCSB PDF and UCLA release: Hadza, San and Tsimane, 94 people with wrist monitors, 5.7-7.1 hours of sleep, sleep onset about 3.3 h after sunset; insomnia so rare that San and Tsimane have no word for it.
- Wording safeguard: 'Between about six and seven hours', 'no word for it in two of the groups'.

### Hill et al. (2011). Co-residence patterns in hunter-gatherer societies show unique human social structure. Science 331, 1286-1289
- How verified: Searched 2026-10-29; Science abstract via PubMed and ASU coverage: 32 foraging societies, about 5,000 people, average band about 28 adults, most band members not close genetic relatives.
- Wording safeguard: 'About twenty-eight adults', 'most not close relatives'; loneliness in the past is not claimed to be zero.

### WHO Commission on Social Connection (2025). From loneliness to social connection (1 in 6 people lonely; about 871,000 deaths a year linked)
- How verified: Searched 2026-10-29; WHO Commission page and 30 June 2025 news item: 1 in 6 people worldwide lonely; loneliness linked to about 871,000 deaths a year.
- Wording safeguard: 'Linked to', not 'causes'.

## Claims softened or dropped
- 'Average adult died at 72' (Gurven & Kaplan): only from forum readings, dropped; 'most common age around seventy' used.
- Raichlen 2017 activity: press gave ~75 min/day, abstract ~135 min/day; 'over two hours a day' follows the abstract.
- Agta leisure: one modern group; the authors' caution against extrapolating is in the narration.
- Tsimane hearts: observational, 'a link, not proof'; worms and infection burden stated.
- Violence rates in prehistory: strongly debated, not used as a round.
- 'Scientists say borrow the good parts' is a general framing of the mismatch literature, kept non-specific.
